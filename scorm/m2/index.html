How this is calculated
  • Distance travelled during the reaction time: speed × reaction time. The car is not slowing at all yet.
  • Braking distance: speed² ÷ (2 × friction × 9.81). Friction is taken as 0.7 on dry asphalt and 0.4 on wet.
  • Risk of death for a pedestrian is interpolated from Tefft (2013, AAA Foundation), all ages. Children and older people are more vulnerable than this average.
  • Real cars, tyres and road surfaces vary. The model is deliberately simple, and generous to the driver.
