Where do the numbers come from?
  • Car lane: 1,200 people/h
  • Bus lane: 6,000 people/h
  • Bike lane: 7,500 people/h

These are mid-range values from street design guides such as NACTO's, which quote ranges (a car lane roughly 600–1,600, a bus lane 4,000–8,000). Real capacity depends on junctions, bus frequency and occupancy, so treat the totals as orders of magnitude, not forecasts. Pavements are not given a capacity here; they are judged on width.
