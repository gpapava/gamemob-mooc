Assumptions
  • Walking 4.8 km/h. Cycling 14 km/h, plus 1 minute to lock up (3 if there is nowhere obvious to park). The bus averages 18 km/h in traffic, 24 when roads are free, with 5 minutes to walk to the stop and 3 at the end; the wait is half the interval.
  • Car: 1 minute to reach it, 22 km/h average in the rush hour (30 when free), then looking for a place to park (4 minutes in the rush, 2 when free) and 3 minutes' walk.
  • These are plausible urban averages, not measurements. Move the sliders: the point is the shape of the lines, not the exact crossing.
