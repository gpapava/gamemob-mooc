Please note

This role-play uses cards and a stopwatch only. No cameras or phones that film people. Do it in the classroom or the hall, not near a real road.

Part A. Human signal role-play

Set up a pretend road across the room. Roles: signal (holds up green or red cards), pedestrians (queue at the kerb), cars (queue at the line), timer (stopwatch) and recorder. Run each controller for 3 minutes. Pedestrians arrive when a teacher or a dice says so (roll a die each 10 seconds: 5 or 6 means one pedestrian arrives; 4, 5 or 6 means one car).

ControllerRule for the signalAverage pedestrian wait (s)Average car wait (s)
Fixed timerPedestrians get green for 10 s every 40 s, whatever happens
Push buttonA pedestrian presses a card when they arrive; green follows after at least 20 s of car green
SensorThe signal can see who is waiting. Go early if no car is waiting; keep cars going if no pedestrian is waiting
