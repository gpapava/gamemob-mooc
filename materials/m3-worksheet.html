Class commute survey

Find out how your class gets to school, turn it into grams of CO₂ and design a plan to cut it.

Privacy

Do not write names or addresses. Count by hands up and by distance band only. Nobody has to answer, and no one should be compared with anyone else.

Part A. Count (hands up)

Ask the class to put their hands up for the mode they usually use, one band at a time. Write the numbers.

Factors are rounded teaching values. Typical distance for each band: 0.6 km, 2 km and 4.5 km.

Part B. Calculate

For each mode and band: pupils × distance × factor. Add them up to get grams for one trip. Then: × 2 trips a day × 180 school days ÷ 1,000 = kg a year.

Part C. Plan

  1. Which band causes the most emissions? Is it the band with the most pupils?
  1. Choose two measures from this list, or invent your own: walking bus, bike train, car pool, school bus, safer crossing, secure bike parking, no-idling rule. For each, say which band it helps.
  1. If your two measures worked, how many pupils would change mode, and what would the new yearly total be? Show your calculation.
