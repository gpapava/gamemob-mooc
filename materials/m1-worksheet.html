Street audit

Measure a real street near school, count who uses it, and compare how the space is shared with how people travel.

Safety first

Work on the pavement or in the school grounds, with an adult. Measure only the part of the street you can reach from the pavement. Do not step into the road. If you cannot go outside, use the plan or photo your teacher gives you.

Part A. How is the space shared?

Pace or measure the width of each strip, from one building line to the other. Write the type (pavement, parking, car lane, bus lane, bike lane, planter, other) and the width in metres. Then calculate its share: width ÷ total width × 100.
